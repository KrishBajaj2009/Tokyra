METHODOLOGY

Measured, not marketed.

Tokyra separates compression quality from simple text deletion.

Compression Ratio

Measures the percentage of tokens removed from the original prompt.

1 - optimized/original

Fidelity Score

Measures preservation of:

✓ Requirements
✓ Constraints
✓ Numbers
✓ Formatting
✓ Intent

CFS Score

Combined Fidelity Score balances compression gains with instruction preservation.

CFS = Compression × Fidelity
