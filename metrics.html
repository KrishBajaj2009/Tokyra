How we score it

Two numbers, combined one way.

A shorter prompt is only a win if it still does the job. We score every compression on both axes and combine them so neither can be gamed alone.

01 — Compression

CR

How much shorter the rewrite is. CR = 1 − (tokens after / tokens before). Higher means more cut — but cutting everything scores 1.0 too, which is why CR never stands alone.

02 — Fidelity

F

How much of the original's meaning and hard constraints survive — exact numbers, thresholds, emails, named entities, negations. Checked against the original, not self-graded by the model that did the compressing.

03 — Combined

CFS

The harmonic mean of CR and F: 2·CR·F / (CR+F). Weighted so a strong score requires both — a compressor can't inflate CFS just by deleting more.

A verified example

One run, hand-checked in full.

A customer-support system prompt, run through the live compressor, with the output checked against seven explicit hard constraints from the original — not sampled, not summarized, this is the actual pair.

Original — 1,028 characters

Please could you kindly act as a helpful and friendly customer support assistant for our company... A few important rules to follow at all times: responses must never exceed 150 words. Always include the support email address, help@northstargear.com... Do not offer refunds for any order placed more than 30 days ago — instead, direct the customer to our warranty page. If a customer mentions the product name "TrailForge 2.0," always ask for their order number... Never make promises about shipping times faster than 5 business days.

Tokyra rewrite — 304 characters

Act as a polite, professional customer support assistant. Provide detailed answers ≤150 words. Include help@northstargear.com if unresolved. No refunds for orders >30 days → warranty page. For "TrailForge 2.0" ask for order number first (batch recall). Never promise shipping faster than 5 business days.

Estimated at ~4 characters per token −70% fewer tokens
CR 70% F 89% CFS 79 / 100

F here means: of 9 atomic facts in the original (word limit, email, refund window, product name, recall trigger, shipping floor, etc.), 8 survived exactly. The one that didn't — the specific recall month — wasn't operational in the instructions to begin with, so it cost fidelity points without costing behavior. That distinction is why we check facts individually instead of eyeballing "does this look about right."

What this page isn't

The honest state of the data.

Tokyra is pre-launch. The live numbers above reflect actual API usage since scoring went live — at low volume, that's a handful of runs, not a benchmark suite. We're not going to round that up with a headline stat that implies more than we've measured.

As usage grows, this page grows with it — same live feed, more data behind it. If you want the methodology in more depth, or you're evaluating Tokyra against another tool and want the raw numbers, email us and we'll share what we have.
