How we score it

Three metrics. One honest measurement.

Tokyra does not measure success by deleting text. A good compression keeps the original intent while removing unnecessary tokens.

01 — Compression Ratio

CR

Measures how many tokens are removed.

CR = 1 - (tokens after / tokens before)

Higher means more compression.

02 — Fidelity

F

Measures whether important information survives.

Numbers, dates, restrictions, formatting requirements, and intent are checked.

03 — Combined Score

CFS

A balanced score combining compression and fidelity.

2 × CR × F / (CR + F)
